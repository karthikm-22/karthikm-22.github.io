## The system

The hardware acquires PPG and ECG signals and transmits them wirelessly to a mobile application. The aim is to classify cardiac disease and monitor blood pressure without a cuff, using signals that a small wearable device can capture.

## ICEdge 2025

The system was presented at the R&D Forum of ICEdge 2025, IISc Bengaluru, in December 2025. Karthik co-authored the poster and demonstrated the system live. The project was selected among the Top 15 projects showcased at the event.
