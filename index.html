Research interest

How much of an algorithm's suitability for real-time, low-power deployment is determined not only by the algorithm itself, but also by the architectural choices made in translating it into hardware.

I am interested in VLSI DSP architectures and ASIC design, where throughput, power, and area constraints are treated as first-order design inputs rather than downstream implementation details.
